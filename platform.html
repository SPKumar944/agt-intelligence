One engine.
Four questions.

Every expansion decision comes down to the same four questions. AGT Intelligence answers each with its own module — built on one shared engine, one hexagonal grid and one set of sources, so the answers agree with each other.

02What will this site earn — and why?

01 · Dynamic catchments

Trade areas that follow real behaviour.

Built from where visitors live and work, on real travel times, respecting rivers, rail lines and expressways.

  • Home and work origins from aggregated mobility
  • Walk, drive and transit isochrones
  • Primary, secondary and tertiary zones by share of customers

80%of customers inside the trade-area contour — not a circle

02 · Revenue forecasting

A forecast you can open, driver by driver.

A site score to rank candidates and a revenue forecast to size them — both decomposed into weighted drivers, with a P10–P90 range.

  • Weights visible and adjustable
  • Quantile forecasts, back-tested on held-out stores
  • Every prediction explained
Open Revenue forecasting
P10–P90a range for every forecast, not false precision

03 · Cannibalisation

Know what a new store takes from your own.

Huff-model store choice before and after an opening, giving overlap, sales transfer in rupees and net incremental sales.

  • Transfer from every existing store at once
  • Competitors in the choice set
  • Spacing and size scenarios
Open Cannibalisation
₹sales transfer and net new sales — not just overlapping circles

04 · Whitespace

Find the gaps before your competitors do.

A metro-wide scan, hexagon by hexagon, for pockets where your target customers are concentrated and your category is absent.

  • Custom target-customer definitions
  • Coverage by your stores and competitors
  • Ranked pockets with search briefs

Metrothe whole market scanned, not one address at a time
