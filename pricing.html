What is a credit?

One credit. One location. One report.

A credit is the unit we use to keep pricing simple. You spend one credit for every location you ask us to analyse, whatever the size of the business.

New account+2 credits added automatically, once, when you first sign in with your Gmail address.
Submitting a request−1 credit per location. A request with three Plus codes uses three credits. They are taken when you submit.
Withdrawing a requestCredits returned. If you withdraw before our analysis starts, every credit comes back to your account.
If we can't take it onCredits returned. If we reject a request, the credits are returned and we tell you why.
Buying moreChoose how many credits you want, pay using the instructions we send you, and we add them once the payment is confirmed.
Larger programmesMore than 10 locations in a request, or locations in several cities, is an enterprise report. Send an enquiry from your account and we will agree the scope and price with you.

Questions

Credits & billing.

Do the free credits expire or renew?

Free credits are a one-time starter allowance per account. They don't renew. They stay in your account until you use them.

What counts as a location?

Each Google Plus code you submit is one location, whether it is an outlet you already run or a site you are evaluating.

How do I pay?

Place an order for the number of credits you want. We email you payment details with an order reference. Once we confirm your payment, the credits appear in your account, usually within one working day.

What if I need a refund?

Credits for a request you withdraw before the analysis starts, or for a request we reject, go straight back to your account. If something goes wrong with a report, contact us and we will put it right.

What is in an enterprise report?

A detailed report for every location, with extended analysis and written findings, for programmes of more than 10 locations or across several cities. Scope and price are agreed with you in advance.

Which cities do you cover?

Standard reports are available for Bengaluru. Other cities are handled as enterprise projects because they need extra data preparation.
