Grocery & convenience

Supermarkets, convenience stores, specialty food

What drives performance
  • Household density within a short walk or drive
  • Daily and weekly trip patterns, evening peaks
  • Parking and walk-in access
  • Competing stores and quick-commerce dark stores
Questions we answer
  • How many households does each store really serve?
  • Where will a new store take share from rivals rather than from us?
  • Which neighbourhoods are under-served for our price point?

QSR, cafés & dining

Quick service, coffee, casual dining, cloud kitchens

What drives performance
  • Footfall during the format's dayparts, not the daily total
  • Office, college and leisure anchors
  • Frontage visibility on the busiest stretch
  • Delivery radius demand alongside walk-in
Questions we answer
  • Which frontage in this area has the most people at lunch or in the evening?
  • How far apart should outlets be?
  • Where would a delivery-only kitchen add orders without cannibalising stores?

Apparel & lifestyle

Fashion, footwear, beauty, electronics, home

What drives performance
  • Spending power and age profile of the trade area
  • Co-tenancy with complementary brands
  • Weekend and evening destination traffic
  • Mall versus high-street dynamics
Questions we answer
  • Mall or high street for this city?
  • Which co-tenants lift our sales, and which compete?
  • What revenue range should we expect in year one?

Pharmacy & health

Pharmacies, diagnostics, clinics, optical

What drives performance
  • Household mix, including older residents and families
  • Proximity to clinics and hospitals
  • Convenience on daily routes
  • Density of competing chains and independents
Questions we answer
  • Which hospitals generate prescription traffic we can capture?
  • Where are families under-served by diagnostics?
  • How close can two of our pharmacies be?

Banking & services

Branches, ATMs, telecom, service centres

What drives performance
  • Workplaces and small-business density
  • Residential income and life stage
  • Overlap between existing branches
  • Transit access for service visits
Questions we answer
  • Which branches overlap enough to consolidate?
  • Where should a new branch or service centre go?
  • How does the network's coverage change after a closure?

Entertainment & leisure

Cinemas, gyms, family entertainment, hospitality

What drives performance
  • Drive-time and transit reach on evenings and weekends
  • Young adult and family populations
  • Dining and retail anchors nearby
  • Competing venues by tier
Questions we answer
  • How large is the 20-minute weekend catchment?
  • Which anchors extend dwell time?
  • Where is the next under-served suburb?

Fuel & automotive

Fuel retail, EV charging, service and dealerships

What drives performance
  • Traffic volume and direction by hour
  • Route position: commuter, highway, destination
  • Competitor spacing along corridors
  • Dwell-time anchors for charging
Questions we answer
  • Which side of the road captures the evening commute?
  • Where are gaps in charging coverage?
  • What volume should a new site expect?

Developers & landlords

Malls, high-street portfolios, mixed-use

What drives performance
  • Catchment size and spending power
  • Tenant mix and co-tenancy effects
  • Footfall by entrance, floor and hour
  • Competing centres
Questions we answer
  • Which tenants fit this catchment, and at what rent-to-sales ratio?
  • How strong is our leasing evidence for a prospective tenant?
  • Where should the next centre be?
