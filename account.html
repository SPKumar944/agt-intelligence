What is a credit?

One credit pays for one location.

  • Each Google Plus code in a request uses 1 credit.
  • Free accounts get 2 credits, once — enough for 2 locations in total.
  • Withdraw a request before our analysis starts, or have it rejected, and the credits come back.
  • Need more? Standard credits are ₹1,000 each. Programmes of 11+ locations or several cities are enterprise (₹8,000 per location).
