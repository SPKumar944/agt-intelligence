Engagement FAQ

Before we talk.

What do you need from us to start?

A list of your stores and candidate sites (addresses, coordinates or plus codes) and a description of the format. Store-level sales are optional but turn scores into calibrated revenue forecasts.

How long does a study take?

The engine produces results for a set of sites quickly; most of an engagement is spent agreeing the business profile and reviewing results with you. We give a timeline in the proposal.

Do we need any software?

No. You get access to the web platform and site reports; data exports work with the GIS, BI and spreadsheet tools you already use.

Which cities can you cover?

Our models are built and proven in Bengaluru and extend to any metro area where the underlying data is available. We confirm coverage for your markets in the first call.

Can we start small?

Yes. Many clients start with a handful of candidate sites or one module — often catchments or cannibalisation — and expand from there.
